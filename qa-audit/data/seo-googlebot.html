07 — Sound

What you hear,and what you don't.

  1. 01

    Adaptive noise cancelling

    Eight microphones listen to the room 50 000 times a second and cancel up to 42 dB of it.

  2. 02

    Beryllium drivers

    A 40 mm dome stiff enough to stay perfectly still at the edges — clean bass, no smear.

  3. 03

    Spatial audio

    Head-tracked sound that stays put when you turn, so a film still sounds like a room.

  4. 04

    Lossless over the air

    24-bit / 96 kHz over Bluetooth 5.4 with LDAC and aptX Lossless.

  5. 05

    Sixty hours

    Two and a half days of playback with noise cancelling on. Ten minutes on the cable gives five hours.
